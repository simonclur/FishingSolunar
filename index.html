FishingSolunar

Weather/wave/wind data covers roughly 3 months back to ~16 days ahead of today. Tide/moon/solunar work for any date.

All rows always show on screen; choose which also print on the laminated sheet.

Open settings (⚙) to choose a location and start date, then click Update.

Data: Open-Meteo (weather, waves, sea temp) · WorldTides (tides) · moon phase & solunar rating computed locally. Cross-check tides against the BOM tide table PDF. See docs for details.
